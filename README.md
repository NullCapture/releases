# Privacy AI Assistant

**Keep your private windows out of screen shares and recordings on Windows, and get instant AI help while you work or meet.**

Privacy AI Assistant is a Windows app by [Null Capture](https://github.com/NullCapture). Choose the windows you want kept private, and they stay hidden from Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet, Discord, OBS and screen recorders. Ask the built-in AI about what is on your screen or being said in a meeting.

## Download

Get the latest version from the **[Releases page](https://github.com/NullCapture/releases/releases/latest)**.

| File | Best for |
|---|---|
| `PrivacyAI-<version>.msi` | Most people. Installs in a minute, no administrator rights, updates itself. |
| `PrivacyAI-portable-v<version>.zip` | Running without installing. Extract and start. |

**Requires:** 64-bit Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11.

Each release includes a `SHA256SUMS` file. To check your download, run `Get-FileHash <file> -Algorithm SHA256` in PowerShell and compare the result.

## What you can do

- **Hide windows from screen sharing.** Pick a window once and it stays private in every call and recording.
- **Ask the AI about your screen.** Get answers based on what you are looking at.
- **Get help in meetings.** Turn on audio to have the conversation transcribed on your own computer, then ask for a summary or a suggested reply.
- **Stay in control.** Nothing is sent until you press send, sensitive text is removed first, and a log shows exactly what was sent.
- **Set a spending limit.** A daily cap on AI cost.
