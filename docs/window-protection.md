# Hiding windows

Choose windows once and they stay private whenever you share your screen or record.

## Choose windows

- **From a list:** **Settings → Windows**, tick the windows.
- **By clicking:** press the crosshair button, then click the window.
- **By shortcut:** click a window and press `Ctrl+Shift+P`.

Protected windows appear normal to you. To others they appear blank.

## Works with

Zoom, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet (in Chrome), Discord, OBS, the Snipping Tool, Xbox Game Bar and the Windows 11 screen recorder.

## After a restart

Your choices are remembered. After you sign in, a window is protected again as soon as its app opens. Until then the status shows **pending**.

## If a window stops being protected

- The app may have been closed and reopened with a different window title. Choose a broader match in **Settings → Windows**.
- After an app update, remove the window from the list and add it again.
- Apps that run as administrator need Privacy AI Assistant to run as administrator too. The app offers this when needed.

## The app's own windows

The floating bar, panel and settings are always hidden from screen sharing. You don't need to add them.

## What it does not cover

See [Limits](limitations.md). In short: cameras pointed at your screen, hardware capture devices, remote desktop and virtual machines can still see everything.
