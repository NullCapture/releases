# Using the AI assistant

Ask a question and get an answer that uses what is on your screen and, if you turned audio on, what has been said in your meeting.

## Ask

1. Click the floating bar to open the panel.
2. Type your question.
3. Press `Ctrl+Enter` or click **Send**.

Answers appear in the panel, which is hidden from screen sharing. You can stop, regenerate or copy any answer.

## Choose what is included

- **Screen** toggle: include the text on your active window.
- **Transcript:** if audio is on, what was said since your last question is included.
- **Screenshot:** only if you press the screenshot button before sending. Never automatic.

## Quick actions

Buttons in the panel give a summary, a suggested reply, or a short answer. Each one sends only when you press it.

## Nothing is sent until you press send

The app reads your screen and audio locally so it is ready when you ask. Nothing leaves your computer until you send. Before sending, passwords, card numbers and keys are removed from the text. See [Privacy](privacy.md).

## Cost

A typical question costs one or two US cents with mainstream models. Set a **daily spending cap** in **Settings → Providers**; the assistant stops when you reach it and tells you.

## Your AI provider

Add your own provider key in **Settings → Providers**, or use the AI included with your license. Choose the model in the same place.
