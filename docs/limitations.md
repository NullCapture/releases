# Limits: what this does not cover

Hiding windows works against screen sharing and recording software running on your computer. It does not stop:

- **A camera or phone** pointed at your screen.
- **Hardware capture devices** that record the video signal.
- **Remote desktop and virtual machines**, which may show everything.
- **Malware or monitoring software** running on your computer, including workplace monitoring installed by an employer.
- **Exam and proctoring software**, which may detect or block tools like this.

Run a test call before relying on it in an important one.

## Other limits

- AI answers can be wrong. Check anything important.
- Speech transcription is not perfect, especially with accents, noise or several people talking at once.
- Antivirus programs sometimes flag apps that work with other windows. If yours does, report it to the antivirus vendor as a false positive.

## Rules

Consent laws for recording, and employer or school policies, are your responsibility. If you are not allowed to use a tool like this, don't.
