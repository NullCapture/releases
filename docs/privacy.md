# Privacy and your data

## You decide what is sent

Nothing goes to an AI provider until you press send. The app reads your screen and audio locally so it is ready, but sends nothing outside your computer until you ask a question.

## See exactly what was sent

**Settings → Privacy → What was sent** lists every request, when it was sent, what triggered it, and the exact text. If the list is empty for a period, nothing left your computer in that period.

## Sensitive text is removed first

Passwords, card numbers, keys and similar are removed from text before it is sent. Adjust the categories in **Settings → Privacy**.

## Apps that are never read

Password managers, banking and sign-in apps are on a blocklist by default. Edit it in **Settings → Privacy**.

## Your keys

Provider keys are kept in Windows secure storage, never in plain files.

## How long data is kept

| Data | Kept | Delete now |
|---|---|---|
| Transcripts | 7 days | Settings → Privacy → Purge |
| Sent log | 7 days | Settings → Privacy → Purge |
| Conversation history | 7 days | Settings → Privacy → Purge |
| Logs | 7 days | Automatic |

Uninstalling offers to remove your data too.

## What connects to the internet

- Your chosen AI provider, only when you send.
- The Releases page, to check for updates (the installed version).
- A one-time download of the speech model.
- The license service, if your license uses one.

Audio recordings are never saved to disk.
