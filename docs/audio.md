# Meetings and audio

Turn on audio to have your meetings transcribed, so the assistant knows what was just said.

## Turn it on

**Settings → Audio**. It is off by default, needs its own confirmation, and you can choose your microphone, the sound from your computer, or both.

While audio is on, an indicator is always visible.

## How it works

- Speech is turned into text **on your computer**. Your voice is never sent anywhere and is never saved.
- Only the text is used, and only when you press send.
- The first time, a speech model downloads once and is kept for later.
- A recent graphics card speeds it up. Without one it still works, a little slower.

## Speed

Text appears a moment after someone stops speaking. Smaller speech models are faster but less accurate. Change the model in **Settings → Audio**.

## Consent

Recording other people may require their consent. See the notice on the [home page](../README.md).
